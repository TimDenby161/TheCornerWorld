Places to visit in Peru

  • Cusco

    The old Inca capital, high in the Andes, and the base for the Sacred Valley and Machu Picchu. Give yourself time to adjust to the altitude.

    Airport: Alejandro Velasco Astete International Airport (CUZ) 3 km

  • Inca Trail

    The classic four-day trek over mountain passes to arrive at Machu Picchu at dawn. Permits sell out months ahead.

    Airport: Alejandro Velasco Astete International Airport (CUZ) 78 km

  • Salkantay Trek

    A five-day alternative route to Machu Picchu, over a high pass beneath a glaciated peak, with no permit needed.

    Airport: Alejandro Velasco Astete International Airport (CUZ) 70 km

  • Peruvian Amazon

    Rainforest lodges up the river from Puerto Maldonado, with macaws, monkeys, caimans and giant otters.

    Airport: Padre Aldamiz International Airport (PEM) 5 km

  • Arequipa

    A city of white volcanic stone beneath three volcanoes, and the way to the condors of Colca Canyon.

    Airport: Rodríguez Ballón International Airport (AQP) 8 km

  • Lima

    The capital on the Pacific cliffs, where most trips begin, known above all for its food.

    Airport: Jorge Chávez International Airport (LIM) 9 km
