Places to visit in Taiwan

  • Taipei

    Night markets, the Taipei 101 tower and one of the world's great museums of Chinese art.

    Airport: Taipei Songshan International Airport (TSA) 4 km

  • Hualien

    A small east-coast city between the mountains and the Pacific, and the gateway to Taroko Gorge.

    Airport: Hualien Chiashan Airport (HUN) 4 km

  • Kaohsiung

    A warm southern port city with a redeveloped harbour, temples on a lotus lake and good seafood.

    Airport: Kaohsiung International Airport (KHH) 8 km

  • Taichung

    A relaxed city of museums and cafes, where bubble tea began, and the way to Sun Moon Lake.

    Airport: Taichung International Airport / Ching Chuang Kang Air Base (RMQ) 14 km
