Places to visit in Uzbekistan

  • Samarkand

    The Registan's three tiled madrasas, and the tombs and mosques of Tamerlane's capital.

    Airport: Samarkand International Airport (SKD) 6 km

  • Bukhara

    A whole old town of mosques, madrasas and trading domes, still lived in.

    Airport: Bukhara International Airport (BHK) 5 km

  • Khiva

    A walled desert city kept like an open-air museum, best at sunset from the walls.

    Airport: Urgench International Airport (UGC) 33 km

  • Tashkent

    The capital: Soviet-era metro stations, a big bazaar and the start of the fast train to Samarkand.

    Airport: Tashkent International Airport (TAS) 6 km
