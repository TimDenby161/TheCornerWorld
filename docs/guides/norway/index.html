Places to visit in Norway

  • Reine

    A fishing village in the Lofoten Islands, ringed by sharp peaks rising out of the sea.

    Airports: Leknes Airport (LKN) 33 km · Bodø Airport (BOO) 91 km

  • Bergen

    Painted wooden wharves, a fish market and seven hills, and the gateway to the western fjords.

    Airport: Bergen Airport, Flesland (BGO) 12 km

  • Oslo

    A waterfront capital with an opera house you can walk on, Viking ships and forest at the end of the metro.

    Airport: Oslo-Gardermoen International Airport (OSL) 37 km

  • Alesund

    An art nouveau town spread over islands, with the Geirangerfjord within reach.

    Airport: Ålesund Airport (AES) 10 km

  • Svolvaer

    The main town of the Lofoten Islands, with ferries, boat trips to Trollfjord and the peaks behind.

    Airports: Svolvær Airport, Helle (SVJ) 4 km · Harstad/Narvik Airport (EVE) 91 km

  • Henningsvaer

    A Lofoten fishing village spread over small islands, with galleries, cafes and a famous football pitch.

    Airports: Svolvær Airport, Helle (SVJ) 22 km · Bodø Airport (BOO) 98 km

  • Bodo

    A small city north of the Arctic Circle, and where the ferry leaves for the Lofoten Islands.

    Airport: Bodø Airport (BOO) 2 km

  • Å

    The last village on the Lofoten road, with red fishing cabins and a stockfish museum.

    Airports: Leknes Airport (LKN) 40 km · Bodø Airport (BOO) 90 km

  • Leknes

    The practical centre of Lofoten, with an airport and the beaches of Haukland and Uttakleiv nearby.

    Airports: Leknes Airport (LKN) 0 km · Bodø Airport (BOO) 103 km

  • Mortsund

    A quiet Lofoten fishing hamlet of cabins on the water, a short drive from Leknes.

    Airports: Leknes Airport (LKN) 8 km · Bodø Airport (BOO) 95 km
