Places to visit in France

  • Paris

    Museums, cafes and long walks along the Seine.

    Airport: Paris-Orly Airport (ORY) 15 km

  • Lille

    Flemish squares, a big art museum and good beer, an hour north of Paris by train.

    Airport: Lille Airport (LIL) 8 km

  • Val Thorens

    The highest ski resort in Europe, at the top of the huge Three Valleys area, with snow from November to May.

    Airports: Chambéry Aix les Bains airport (CMF) 66 km · Lyon Saint-Exupéry Airport (LYS) 125 km

Good to know

  • High-speed trains make most of the country reachable from Paris in a few hours.
