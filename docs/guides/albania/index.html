Places to visit in Albania

  • Tirana

    A colourful, fast-changing capital, with Cold War bunkers turned into museums.

    Airport: Tirana International Airport Mother Teresa (TIA) 13 km

  • Theth

    A remote village in the Albanian Alps, reached by a mountain road and ringed by peaks.

    Airport: Tirana International Airport Mother Teresa (TIA) 110 km

  • Ksamil

    White sand and small islands in clear water, in the far south beside the ruins of Butrint.

    Airport: Tirana International Airport Mother Teresa (TIA) 184 km

  • Kruja Castle

    The hilltop stronghold of the national hero Skanderbeg, above an old bazaar street.

    Airport: Tirana International Airport Mother Teresa (TIA) 12 km

  • Shiroka

    A fishing village on the shore of Lake Shkodra, known for its lakeside fish restaurants.

    Airport: Tirana International Airport Mother Teresa (TIA) 75 km
