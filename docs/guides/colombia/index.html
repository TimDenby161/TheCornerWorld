Places to visit in Colombia

  • Cartagena

    A walled colonial city on the Caribbean, with bright balconied streets and islands offshore.

    Airport: Rafael Nuñez International Airport (CTG) 7 km

  • Medellin

    The city of eternal spring, in a green valley, known for its cable cars, street art and nightlife.

    Airports: Jose Maria Córdova International Airport (MDE) 19 km · Enrique Olaya Herrera Airport (EOH) 3 km

  • Bogota

    A high-altitude capital with the Gold Museum, the old quarter of La Candelaria and a cable car up Monserrate.

    Airport: El Dorado International Airport (BOG) 9 km

  • Guatape

    A lakeside town of brightly painted houses, beside a giant rock with 700 steps to the top. A day trip from Medellin.

    Airport: Jose Maria Córdova International Airport (MDE) 30 km
