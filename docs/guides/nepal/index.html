Places to visit in Nepal

  • Kathmandu

    Temple squares, stupas and crowded bazaars, and where almost every trip to Nepal begins.

    Airport: Tribhuvan International Airport (KTM) 5 km

  • Pokhara

    A relaxed lakeside town under the Annapurna range, and the start of its treks.

    Airports: Pokhara International Airport (PHH) 4 km · Pokhara Domestic Airport (PKR) 1 km

  • Everest Base Camp

    The classic trek through Sherpa villages to the foot of the world's highest mountain. About twelve days from the airstrip at Lukla.

    Airports: Tenzing-Hillary Airport (LUA) 37 km · Tribhuvan International Airport (KTM) 150 km

  • Annapurna Base Camp

    A trek from near Pokhara up through rhododendron forest into a ring of peaks. About a week to ten days.

    Airports: Pokhara International Airport (PHH) 41 km · Pokhara Domestic Airport (PKR) 38 km
