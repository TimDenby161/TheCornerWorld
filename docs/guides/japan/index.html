Places to visit in Japan

  • Tokyo

    A vast city of distinct neighbourhoods, from Shibuya's crossing to Asakusa's temples.

    Airport: Tokyo Haneda International Airport (HND) 15 km

  • Kyoto

    The old imperial capital: hundreds of temples, the gates of Fushimi Inari and the geisha district of Gion.

    Airports: Kansai International Airport (KIX) 81 km · Osaka Itami International Airport (ITM) 39 km

  • Osaka

    Japan's kitchen: street food and neon in Dotonbori, a castle and an easy-going feel.

    Airports: Kansai International Airport (KIX) 37 km · Osaka Itami International Airport (ITM) 11 km

  • Mount Fuji

    Japan's highest and most famous mountain, seen from the Five Lakes or Hakone and climbed in July and August.

    Airport: Mount Fuji Shizuoka Airport (FSZ) 80 km

  • Hiroshima

    The Peace Memorial Park and museum, and the floating shrine gate of Miyajima a short ferry away.

    Airports: Hiroshima Airport (HIJ) 42 km · Iwakuni Kintaikyo Airport (IWK) 33 km

  • Nara

    A giant bronze Buddha in a vast wooden hall, and a park full of tame deer. An easy day from Kyoto or Osaka.

    Airports: Kansai International Airport (KIX) 59 km · Osaka Itami International Airport (ITM) 34 km

  • Hokkaido

    The northern island: powder snow in winter, flower fields and national parks in summer, with Sapporo as the base.

    Airports: New Chitose Airport (CTS) 42 km · Sapporo Okadama Airport (OKD) 7 km

  • Okinawa

    Subtropical islands far to the south, with beaches, coral reefs and a culture of their own.

    Airport: Naha International Airport (OKA) 4 km

  • Nagano

    A mountain city with a great temple, snow monkeys bathing in hot springs nearby and the ski slopes of the Japan Alps.

    Airports: Shinshu-Matsumoto Airport (MMJ) 58 km · Komatsu Airport / JASDF Komatsu Air Base (KMQ) 161 km

  • Fukuoka

    The main city of Kyushu, known for ramen and its evening food stalls along the river.

    Airport: Fukuoka Airport (FUK) 5 km
