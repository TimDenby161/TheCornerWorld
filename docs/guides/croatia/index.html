Places to visit in Croatia

  • Dubrovnik

    A walled city on the sea. Walk the ramparts early, before the cruise ships arrive.

    Airport: Dubrovnik Ruđer Bošković Airport (DBV) 15 km

  • Split

    A Roman emperor's palace that became the old town, and the main port for the islands.

    Airport: Split Saint Jerome Airport (SPU) 12 km

  • Plitvice Lakes

    Sixteen turquoise lakes joined by waterfalls, seen from wooden walkways.

    Airport: Zadar Airport (ZAD) 90 km

  • Hvar

    A sunny island with a handsome harbour town, lavender fields and boat trips to quiet coves.

    Airports: Split Saint Jerome Airport (SPU) 43 km · Brač Airport (BWK) 23 km

  • Zadar

    Roman ruins and old churches on a small peninsula, with an organ on the seafront played by the waves.

    Airport: Zadar Airport (ZAD) 10 km

  • Krka National Park

    A river of wide waterfalls and pools, an easy day trip from Split or Zadar.

    Airport: Split Saint Jerome Airport (SPU) 39 km
