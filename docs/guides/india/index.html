Places to visit in India

  • Delhi

    Mughal monuments, markets, and the usual way in to the north.

    Airport: Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) 13 km

  • Agra

    The Taj Mahal, best at sunrise, and Agra Fort.

    Airports: Agra Airport / Agra Air Force Station (AGR) 5 km · Noida International Airport (DXN) 118 km

  • Jaipur

    The Pink City: Amber Fort, palaces and bazaars.

    Airport: Jaipur International Airport (JAI) 10 km

  • Varanasi

    One of the world's oldest living cities, on the banks of the Ganges.

    Airport: Lal Bahadur Shastri International Airport (VNS) 18 km

  • Goa

    Beaches and Portuguese-era towns on the west coast.

    Airport: Goa Dabolim International Airport (GOI) 32 km

  • Kerala backwaters

    Palm-lined canals and lagoons, travelled by houseboat.

    Airport: Cochin International Airport (COK) 73 km

  • Udaipur

    A city of lakes and white palaces in the hills of southern Rajasthan.

    Airports: Maharana Pratap Airport (UDR) 19 km · Sardar Vallabh Patel International Airport (AMD) 200 km

  • Mumbai

    India's biggest city: colonial-era landmarks, Bollywood, street food and the sea all along one side.

    Airport: Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM) 2 km

  • Ranthambore National Park

    The best-known place in India to see wild tigers, on jeep safaris around a ruined hilltop fort.

    Airport: Jaipur International Airport (JAI) 113 km

  • Ahmedabad

    Gandhi's ashram, carved stepwells and a walled old city of narrow lanes.

    Airport: Sardar Vallabh Patel International Airport (AMD) 9 km
