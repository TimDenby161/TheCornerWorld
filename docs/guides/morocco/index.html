Places to visit in Morocco

  • Marrakesh

    The red city: the great square of Jemaa el-Fna, souks, palaces and gardens, with the Atlas mountains on the horizon.

    Airport: Marrakesh Menara Airport (RAK) 5 km

  • Fez

    The largest medieval old city in the world still lived in, a maze of nine thousand lanes, tanneries and craft workshops.

    Airport: Fes Saïss International Airport (FEZ) 15 km

  • Merzouga

    A village at the foot of the Erg Chebbi dunes, for a camel ride into the Sahara and a night in a desert camp.

    Airports: Moulay Ali Cherif Airport (ERH) 101 km · Zagora Airport (OZG) 200 km

  • Tangier

    A white port city facing Spain across the strait, with a hilltop kasbah and a long literary past.

    Airport: Tangier Ibn Battuta Airport (TNG) 9 km

  • Agadir

    A modern Atlantic resort with a long sandy beach, and surf villages just up the coast.

    Airport: Al Massira Airport (AGA) 22 km
