Places to visit in Scotland

  • Edinburgh

    The castle, the Royal Mile and Arthur's Seat, in a city built for walking.

    Airport: Edinburgh Airport (EDI) 11 km

  • Loch Lomond

    Britain's largest lake, with wooded islands and the first of the Highland hills.

    Airport: Glasgow Airport (GLA) 28 km

  • Cairngorms

    High mountain plateau, pine forest and wildlife, with Aviemore as the base.

    Airports: Inverness Airport (INV) 41 km · Aberdeen International Airport (ABZ) 98 km

  • Inverness

    The small capital of the Highlands, on the doorstep of Loch Ness.

    Airports: Inverness Airport (INV) 12 km · Aberdeen International Airport (ABZ) 125 km

  • Isle of Skye

    The Cuillin mountains, the Old Man of Storr and sea cliffs, reached by a bridge from the mainland.

    Airports: Benbecula Airport (BEB) 70 km · Glasgow Airport (GLA) 202 km

  • Ben Nevis

    Britain's highest mountain, a full day's climb from Fort William at its foot.

    Airports: Glasgow Airport (GLA) 109 km · Inverness Airport (INV) 101 km
