Places to visit in Tanzania

  • Serengeti

    Endless plains and the greatest concentration of big animals in Africa, with the migration moving through all year.

    Airport: Mwanza International Airport (MWZ) 209 km

  • Ngorongoro Crater

    A collapsed volcano whose floor is packed with wildlife, including black rhino, seen on a day's drive from the rim.

    Airports: Arusha Airport (ARK) 127 km · Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) 176 km

  • Zanzibar

    The alleys and carved doors of Stone Town, spice farms, and white-sand beaches for the end of a safari.

    Airport: Abeid Amani Karume International Airport (ZNZ) 8 km

  • Kilimanjaro

    The highest mountain in Africa, climbed without ropes in six to eight days. Treks start from Moshi.

    Airport: Kilimanjaro International Airport (JRO) 31 km

  • Iringa

    A cool highland town on the road south, and the way in to Ruaha National Park.

    Airport: Dodoma Airport (DOD) 178 km
