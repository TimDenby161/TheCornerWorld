Places to visit in Thailand

  • Bangkok

    The Grand Palace, riverside temples, markets and street food, in a city that never slows down.

    Airport: Don Mueang International Airport (DMK) 21 km

  • Chiang Mai

    A walled old city full of temples, with night markets, cooking classes and mountains all round.

    Airport: Chiang Mai International Airport (CNX) 4 km

  • Koh Phi Phi

    Sheer limestone cliffs around turquoise bays, with boat trips to Maya Bay.

    Airport: Krabi International Airport (KBV) 46 km

  • Krabi

    Limestone cliffs straight out of the sea, with Railay Beach and island-hopping by longtail boat.

    Airport: Krabi International Airport (KBV) 9 km

  • Phuket

    Thailand's biggest island. Kata Beach, in the south-west, is one of the more relaxed places to stay.

    Airport: Phuket International Airport (HKT) 33 km

  • Koh Samui

    A large Gulf island with palm-fringed beaches, from busy Chaweng to quieter coves.

    Airport: Samui International Airport (USM) 7 km

  • Koh Tao

    A small island and one of the cheapest and most popular places in the world to learn to dive.

    Airport: Samui International Airport (USM) 66 km

  • Koh Lanta

    A long, laid-back island of quiet beaches and sunsets, with an old fishing town on the east side.

    Airport: Krabi International Airport (KBV) 54 km

  • Khao Sok

    Ancient rainforest and a lake of limestone towers, where you can sleep in a floating bungalow.

    Airports: Phuket International Airport (HKT) 92 km · Surat Thani Airport (URT) 71 km

  • Pai

    A backpacker town in a mountain valley, reached by a road of several hundred bends from Chiang Mai.

    Airports: Chiang Mai International Airport (CNX) 86 km · Mae Hong Son Airport (HGN) 49 km

  • Chiang Rai

    The far north, known for the dazzling White Temple and the Blue Temple.

    Airport: Mae Fah Luang - Chiang Rai International Airport (CEI) 7 km

  • Koh Yao Noi

    A quiet island in Phang Nga Bay, with rice fields, fishing villages and views of the karst islands.

    Airport: Phuket International Airport (HKT) 32 km

  • Koh Yao Yai

    The larger and even sleepier of the Yao islands, between Phuket and Krabi.

    Airport: Phuket International Airport (HKT) 33 km

  • Pattaya

    A big, brash beach resort two hours from Bangkok, with islands just offshore.

    Airport: U-Tapao–Rayong–Pattaya International Airport (UTP) 31 km

  • Surat Thani

    The mainland transport hub for the Gulf islands and Khao Sok.

    Airports: Surat Thani Airport (URT) 21 km · Samui International Airport (USM) 92 km

  • Hat Yai

    The big city of the deep south, known for its markets and food, and the usual stop before Malaysia.

    Airport: Hat Yai International Airport (HDY) 12 km

  • Chiang Khong

    A small Mekong town at the border bridge to Laos, where travellers cross to the slow boat.

    Airport: Mae Fah Luang - Chiang Rai International Airport (CEI) 65 km
