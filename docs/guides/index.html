Travel guides

When to go, what it costs, where to stop and ready-made routes.

  • 🇦🇱AlbaniaDraft Wild mountains in the north, a warm southern coast and a capital full of colour.
  • 🇦🇩AndorraDraft A small mountain country in the Pyrenees, for skiing in winter and walking in summer.
  • 🇦🇷ArgentinaDraft Tango and steak in the capital, then glaciers and mountains at the bottom of the world.
  • 🇦🇲ArmeniaDraft Ancient monasteries in the mountains, around a pink-stone capital.
  • 🇦🇺AustraliaDraft A continent of its own: beach cities, reef and rainforest on the coasts, and the red desert in the middle.
  • 🇦🇹AustriaDraft Alpine valleys and imperial cities.
  • 🇦🇿AzerbaijanDraft An oil-rich capital on the Caspian Sea, with mud volcanoes and mountain villages beyond.
  • 🇧🇾BelarusDraft Wide boulevards, forests and a strong sense of the Soviet past.
  • 🇧🇪BelgiumDraft Grand squares, medieval canals, and beer, chocolate and chips done properly.
  • 🇧🇿BelizeDraft A small English-speaking country on the Caribbean, with a long barrier reef and jungle inland.
  • 🇧🇴BoliviaDraft High plains, salt flats and Andean cities, in one of South America's most striking landscapes.
  • 🇧🇦Bosnia and HerzegovinaDraft Ottoman bridges, mountain rivers and old towns, a short drive inland from the Adriatic.
  • 🇧🇼BotswanaDraft Some of the best wildlife in Africa, in a country that keeps its wilderness uncrowded.
  • 🇧🇷BrazilDraft A country the size of a continent: beach cities, rainforest and some of the world's biggest waterfalls.
  • 🇧🇬BulgariaDraft Old towns, mountain monasteries and a Black Sea coast.
  • 🇰🇭CambodiaDraft The temples of Angkor, a riverside capital, and quiet towns and islands in the south.
  • 🇨🇦CanadaDraft Enormous and mostly wild: mountains and lakes in the west, big cities in the east, and the Arctic to the north.
  • 🇨🇱ChileDraft A long, thin country running from desert in the north to glaciers in the south.
  • 🇨🇳ChinaDraft Imperial palaces, the Great Wall and enormous modern cities.
  • 🇨🇴ColombiaDraft Colonial towns, coffee hills and a Caribbean coast, in a country that has opened up to visitors.
  • 🇭🇷CroatiaDraft A long Adriatic coast of walled towns and islands, with lakes and waterfalls inland.
  • 🇨🇾CyprusDraft A sunny island of beaches, ancient ruins and mountain villages.
  • 🇨🇿CzechiaDraft A fairy-tale capital, and castles and beer towns beyond it.
  • 🇪🇨EcuadorDraft Andean cities, cloud forest and the wildlife of the Galapagos, in one small country.
  • 🇪🇬EgyptDraft Five thousand years of history along the Nile, from the pyramids to the temples of the south.
  • 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁥󠁮󠁧󠁿EnglandDraft From London's museums and theatres to northern cities, Lake District fells and the beaches of the south-west.
  • 🇪🇪EstoniaDraft A medieval capital, forests, bogs and islands.
  • 🇫🇯FijiDraft More than three hundred islands in the South Pacific, with warm water, coral reefs and a famously friendly welcome.
  • 🇫🇮FinlandDraft Design, saunas and lakes in the south, and the Arctic in the north.
  • 🇫🇷FranceDraft Paris is the obvious start, but the country rewards going further: wine regions, the Alps and the Mediterranean coast are all a fast train away.
  • 🇬🇪GeorgiaDraft Old churches, an ancient wine tradition and the high Caucasus, between the Black Sea and the mountains.
  • 🇩🇪GermanyDraft Big cities with plenty of history, and the Alps along the southern edge.
  • 🇬🇮GibraltarDraft A British territory on a limestone rock at the mouth of the Mediterranean.
  • 🇬🇷GreeceDraft Ancient ruins on the mainland and hundreds of islands, each with its own character.
  • 🇬🇹GuatemalaDraft Maya ruins in the jungle, colonial streets, and a highland lake ringed by volcanoes.
  • 🇭🇰Hong KongDraft A dense, vertical city of harbour views, markets and food, with green hills behind.
  • 🇭🇺HungaryDraft A grand capital on the Danube, known for its thermal baths.
  • 🇮🇸IcelandDraft Volcanoes, glaciers, waterfalls and hot springs, around a small and lively capital.
  • 🇮🇳IndiaDraft Vast and varied. Most first trips pick one region: the forts and palaces of the north, or the slower, greener south.
  • 🇮🇩IndonesiaDraft A chain of thousands of islands, from temples and volcanoes on Java to dragons and reefs in the east.
  • 🇮🇪IrelandDraft A sociable capital, then green countryside and a wild Atlantic coast.
  • 🇮🇱IsraelDraft Holy sites, desert and a Mediterranean coast, in a small country.
  • 🇮🇹ItalyDraft Art cities, hill towns, coast and mountains, joined up by fast trains. Works as a city break, a food trip or a full fortnight.
  • 🇯🇵JapanDraft Neon cities, quiet temples and exceptional food, joined up by very fast trains.
  • 🇯🇴JordanDraft A compact country that packs in a rock-cut ancient city, a red-sand desert and the lowest point on Earth. Easy to drive yourself and manageable in a week.
  • 🇰🇿KazakhstanDraft A vast country of steppe, with mountains along its southern edge.
  • 🇰🇪KenyaDraft The classic safari country: open savannah, big cats and the great wildebeest migration.
  • 🇽🇰KosovoDraft Europe's youngest country: Ottoman towns, monasteries and high mountains.
  • 🇰🇬KyrgyzstanDraft High pastures, yurts and alpine lakes in the Tien Shan mountains.
  • 🇱🇦LaosDraft Temples, karst mountains and slow travel along the Mekong.
  • 🇱🇻LatviaDraft A handsome capital, pine forests and long sandy beaches.
  • 🇱🇮LiechtensteinDraft One of the world's smallest countries, squeezed between the Rhine and the Alps.
  • 🇱🇹LithuaniaDraft A baroque capital, forests, lakes and a coast of sand dunes.
  • 🇱🇺LuxembourgDraft A tiny country around a fortress capital, set among wooded valleys.
  • 🇲🇼MalawiDraft A small, friendly country strung along a huge freshwater lake.
  • 🇲🇾MalaysiaDraft Skyscrapers and street food in the capital, with rainforest, islands and Borneo beyond.
  • 🇪🇸MallorcaDraft The largest of the Balearic Islands: a handsome capital, mountains and coves.
  • 🇲🇹MaltaDraft Fortress cities, ancient temples and clear water, on small islands in the middle of the Mediterranean.
  • 🇲🇽MexicoDraft Ancient cities, colonial towns, beaches on two oceans and one of the world's great cuisines.
  • 🇲🇩MoldovaDraft A little-visited country of vineyards, wine cellars and Soviet-era streets.
  • 🇲🇨MonacoDraft A tiny, wealthy city-state on the Riviera, seen in a day.
  • 🇲🇪MontenegroDraft A small country of steep mountains dropping to the Adriatic.
  • 🇲🇦MoroccoDraft Walled medinas, the Atlas mountains and the edge of the Sahara, a short flight from Europe.
  • 🇳🇵NepalDraft The highest mountains on Earth, with temple cities and lakeside towns in the valleys below.
  • 🇳🇱NetherlandsDraft Canals, bicycles and great museums, in a flat country that is easy to get around.
  • 🇳🇿New ZealandDraft Two islands made for a road trip: geothermal valleys and beaches in the north, mountains, fjords and glaciers in the south.
  • 🇰🇵North KoreaDraft The most closed country in the world, seen only on a guided tour.
  • 🇲🇰North MacedoniaDraft A lake town of old churches, a capital of statues and a canyon on its doorstep.
  • 🇬🇧Northern IrelandDraft A city with a shipbuilding past, and a coast road north to basalt cliffs.
  • 🇳🇴NorwayDraft Fjords, mountains and Arctic islands.
  • 🇴🇲OmanDraft Forts, wadis, desert and mountains, in the calmest corner of Arabia.
  • 🇵🇦PanamaDraft The narrow link between two continents and two oceans, with rainforest on both sides of the canal.
  • 🇵🇪PeruDraft Inca ruins in the Andes, the Amazon rainforest and one of the world's great cuisines.
  • 🇵🇭PhilippinesDraft More than seven thousand islands, with some of the clearest water and best beaches in Asia.
  • 🇵🇱PolandDraft Old market squares, a rebuilt capital and a hard twentieth-century history.
  • 🇵🇹PortugalDraft Tiled cities, Atlantic beaches and good food at gentle prices.
  • 🇶🇦QatarDraft A desert peninsula with a fast-growing capital on the Gulf.
  • 🇷🇴RomaniaDraft A big, busy capital, then Transylvanian towns, castles and the Carpathian mountains.
  • 🇷🇺RussiaDraft The largest country on Earth. Most visits centre on its two great cities.
  • 🇸🇲San MarinoDraft A tiny hilltop republic surrounded by Italy.
  • 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁳󠁣󠁴󠁿ScotlandDraft A handsome capital, then lochs, glens and mountains all the way north.
  • 🇷🇸SerbiaDraft A lively capital where two big rivers meet, with monasteries and hills to the south.
  • 🇸🇬SingaporeDraft A small, green and very orderly city-state, with some of the best food in Asia.
  • 🇸🇰SlovakiaDraft A small capital on the Danube, with castles and the Tatra mountains beyond.
  • 🇸🇮SloveniaDraft A small green country of lakes, gorges and mountains, around an easy-going capital.
  • 🇿🇦South AfricaDraft Big game, wine country and dramatic coast, at the southern tip of the continent.
  • 🇰🇷South KoreaDraft A high-energy capital, mountain temples and some of Asia's best food.
  • 🇪🇸SpainDraft Late nights, great food and very different regions, from Catalonia to Andalusia.
  • 🇸🇪SwedenDraft A capital built on islands, then forests, lakes and a long northern wilderness.
  • 🇨🇭SwitzerlandDraft Lakes, mountains and tidy cities, joined up by trains that run to the minute.
  • 🇹🇼TaiwanDraft A mountainous island of night markets, hot springs and marble gorges.
  • 🇹🇯TajikistanDraft A country that is almost all mountains, crossed by one of the world's great road journeys.
  • 🇹🇿TanzaniaDraft Africa's highest mountain, its most famous plains and a spice island in the Indian Ocean.
  • 🇹🇭ThailandDraft Temples and mountains in the north, a huge capital in the middle, and islands and beaches down both coasts.
  • 🇹🇷TürkiyeDraft Where Europe meets Asia: one vast historic city, then ruins, coast and strange landscapes inland.
  • 🇹🇲TurkmenistanDraft One of the least-visited countries in the world: a marble capital and a burning crater in the desert.
  • 🇹🇻TuvaluDraft One of the smallest and least-visited countries in the world: a few low coral atolls in the middle of the Pacific.
  • 🇺🇬UgandaDraft Green hills, great lakes and forests that are home to mountain gorillas and chimpanzees.
  • 🇺🇦UkraineDraft A large country of grand cities, wide steppe and a long history.
  • 🇦🇪United Arab EmiratesDraft Futuristic cities on the Gulf, with desert and mountains behind.
  • 🇺🇸United StatesDraft Too big for one trip. Pick a region: the cities of the east, the national parks of the west, or a road trip through the south.
  • 🇺🇾UruguayDraft A small, relaxed country of beaches, old towns and open ranchland.
  • 🇺🇿UzbekistanDraft The great Silk Road cities, with tiled mosques, madrasas and bazaars.
  • 🇻🇦Vatican CityDraft The world's smallest state, inside Rome.
  • 🇻🇳VietnamDraft A long, thin country best travelled north to south or the reverse: limestone bays, old trading towns, big energetic cities and some of the best street food in Asia.
  • 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁷󠁬󠁳󠁿WalesDraft Mountains, castles and a long, wild coast.
  • 🇵🇸West BankDraft Hill towns and holy sites between Jerusalem and the Jordan valley.
  • 🇿🇲ZambiaDraft Wild, uncrowded national parks and the birthplace of the walking safari.
  • 🇿🇼ZimbabweDraft One of the world's great waterfalls, with national parks and ancient ruins beyond.
