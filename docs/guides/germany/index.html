Places to visit in Germany

  • Berlin

    The Wall, Museum Island and the Brandenburg Gate, in a capital still reinventing itself.

    Airport: Berlin Brandenburg Airport (BER) 19 km

  • Munich

    Beer halls, grand squares and the English Garden, with the Alps an hour south.

    Airport: Munich Airport (MUC) 28 km

  • Hamburg

    A huge working port, brick warehouses on the water and the Elbphilharmonie concert hall.

    Airport: Hamburg Helmut Schmidt Airport (HAM) 9 km
