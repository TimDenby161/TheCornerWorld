Places to visit in Philippines

  • Palawan

    A long, wild island of limestone cliffs and hidden lagoons, with El Nido as the base for island-hopping.

    Airports: Francisco B. Reyes (Busuanga) Airport (USU) 130 km · Puerto Princesa International Airport / PAF Antonio Bautista Air Base (PPS) 174 km

  • Coron

    Jagged islands, clear lakes and some of the best wreck diving in the world, north of Palawan.

    Airports: Francisco B. Reyes (Busuanga) Airport (USU) 17 km · Kalibo International Airport (KLO) 239 km

  • Boracay

    A small island famous for the four kilometres of powdery sand at White Beach.

    Airports: Godofredo P. Ramos Airport (MPH) 6 km · Kalibo International Airport (KLO) 59 km

  • Cebu

    The hub of the central islands: an old Spanish city, with waterfalls, canyoning and diving around the island.

    Airport: Mactan Cebu International Airport (CEB) 10 km
