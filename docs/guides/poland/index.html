Places to visit in Poland

  • Krakow

    A huge medieval market square, Wawel Castle and the old Jewish quarter of Kazimierz.

    Airport: Kraków John Paul II International Airport (KRK) 11 km

  • Auschwitz

    The Auschwitz-Birkenau memorial and museum, a sobering day trip from Krakow. Book ahead.

    Airport: Kraków John Paul II International Airport (KRK) 42 km

  • Warsaw

    A capital rebuilt from rubble after the war, with a restored old town and strong museums.

    Airport: Warsaw Chopin Airport (WAW) 8 km

  • Wroclaw

    A colourful market square, islands and bridges on the Oder, and bronze dwarfs hidden all over town.

    Airport: Copernicus Wrocław Airport (WRO) 10 km

  • Lodz

    A former textile city, with mills turned into museums and shops and one very long main street.

    Airport: Łódź Władysław Reymont Airport (LCJ) 6 km
