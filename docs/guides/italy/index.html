Places to visit in Italy

  • Rome

    The Colosseum, the Forum, the Vatican, and long lunches in between.

    Airport: Ciampino–G. B. Pastine International Airport (CIA) 14 km

  • Florence

    Renaissance art and architecture, and the base for Tuscany.

    Airport: Florence Airport, Peretola (FLR) 6 km

  • Venice

    Canals and back alleys. Stay overnight to see it after the day trippers leave.

    Airport: Venice Marco Polo Airport (VCE) 8 km

  • Cinque Terre

    Five cliffside villages linked by coastal paths and a local train.

    Airport: Pisa International Airport (PSA) 74 km

  • Amalfi Coast

    Steep villages above the sea south of Naples, with Pompeii close by.

    Airports: Naples International Airport (NAP) 39 km · Salerno Costa d'Amalfi Airport (QSR) 26 km

  • Dolomites

    Jagged peaks for summer hiking and winter skiing.

    Airports: Bolzano Airport (BZO) 40 km · Treviso Airport (TSF) 89 km

  • Milan

    The Duomo, Leonardo's Last Supper and the country's fashion and design capital.

    Airport: Milano Linate Airport (LIN) 7 km

  • Naples

    Loud, crowded and full of life: the home of pizza, with Pompeii and Vesuvius next door.

    Airport: Naples International Airport (NAP) 4 km

  • Bologna

    Miles of arcaded streets, medieval towers and some of the best food in Italy.

    Airport: Bologna Guglielmo Marconi Airport (BLQ) 6 km

  • Pisa

    The Leaning Tower and its cathedral square, an easy stop between Florence and the coast.

    Airport: Pisa International Airport (PSA) 4 km

  • Stelvio Pass

    Forty-eight hairpin bends up to one of the highest road passes in the Alps. Open in summer only.

    Airports: Bolzano Airport (BZO) 68 km · Il Caravaggio International Airport (BGY) 111 km

  • Colosseum

    Rome's great amphitheatre, with the Forum and the Palatine Hill beside it on the same ticket.

    Airport: Ciampino–G. B. Pastine International Airport (CIA) 13 km

  • Lake Como

    A long, deep lake under steep mountains, with villas, gardens and ferries between the villages.

    Airport: Il Caravaggio International Airport (BGY) 50 km

  • Lake Garda

    Italy's largest lake: lemon groves and castles in the south, cliffs and windsurfing in the north.

    Airport: Verona Villafranca Valerio Catullo Airport (VRN) 31 km
