Places to visit in Malawi

  • Kande Beach

    A sandy bay on Lake Malawi, for swimming, snorkelling among bright cichlid fish and village visits.

    Airport: Kamuzu International Airport (LLW) 208 km

  • Chitipa

    A small town in the far north, near the borders with Tanzania and Zambia, and a stop for overland travellers.

    Airport: Songwe Airport (MBI) 87 km
