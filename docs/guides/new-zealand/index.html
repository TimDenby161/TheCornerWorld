Places to visit in New Zealand

  • Auckland

    The main arrival city, built around harbours and volcanic cones.

    Airport: Auckland International Airport (AKL) 18 km

  • Rotorua

    Geysers, hot pools and Maori culture.

    Airports: Rotorua Regional Airport (ROT) 7 km · Auckland International Airport (AKL) 180 km

  • Wellington

    The small, walkable capital, and the ferry port for the South Island.

    Airport: Wellington International Airport (WLG) 5 km

  • Abel Tasman

    Golden beaches and a coastal track, on foot or by kayak.

    Airports: Nelson Airport (NSN) 46 km · Wellington International Airport (WLG) 160 km

  • Queenstown

    A lakeside adventure town ringed by mountains.

    Airport: Queenstown Airport (ZQN) 7 km

  • Milford Sound

    A fjord of sheer cliffs and waterfalls, reached by one of the great drives.

    Airport: Queenstown Airport (ZQN) 75 km

  • Mount Cook

    Aoraki, the country's highest peak, with glacier lakes and the Hooker Valley walk from the village at its foot.

    Airports: Timaru Airport (TIU) 110 km · Queenstown Airport (ZQN) 179 km

  • Wanaka

    A lakeside town quieter than Queenstown, with the Roys Peak hike and Mount Aspiring National Park behind.

    Airports: Queenstown Airport (ZQN) 47 km · Wanaka Airport (WKA) 10 km

  • Franz Josef

    A West Coast village below a glacier that drops almost to the rainforest, seen on foot or by helicopter.

    Airports: Hokitika Airfield (HKK) 100 km · Christchurch International Airport (CHC) 190 km

  • Lake Tekapo

    A milky-turquoise lake with a tiny stone church on the shore, under some of the darkest night skies in the world.

    Airports: Timaru Airport (TIU) 68 km · Christchurch International Airport (CHC) 174 km

  • Kaikoura

    A coastal town between mountains and deep ocean, for whale watching, dolphins, seals and crayfish.

    Airports: Woodbourne Airport (BHE) 99 km · Wellington International Airport (WLG) 151 km

  • Christchurch

    The South Island's main city, rebuilt after its earthquakes, with gardens, a river through the centre and the main airport.

    Airport: Christchurch International Airport (CHC) 10 km

  • Taupo

    A town on the country's largest lake, with Huka Falls, hot pools and the Tongariro Alpine Crossing nearby.

    Airports: Taupo Airport (TUO) 6 km · Auckland International Airport (AKL) 218 km

  • Te Anau

    A lakeside town on the edge of Fiordland, and the base for Milford Sound and the Kepler and Milford tracks.

    Airport: Queenstown Airport (ZQN) 91 km

  • Waiheke

    An island of vineyards, olive groves and beaches, forty minutes by ferry from Auckland.

    Airport: Auckland International Airport (AKL) 37 km

  • Arthur's Pass

    A mountain village on the road and railway across the Southern Alps, with short walks to waterfalls and cheeky kea.

    Airports: Hokitika Airfield (HKK) 53 km · Christchurch International Airport (CHC) 100 km

  • Hokitika

    A West Coast beach town known for greenstone carving, with the turquoise Hokitika Gorge inland.

    Airports: Hokitika Airfield (HKK) 2 km · Christchurch International Airport (CHC) 154 km

  • Fairlie

    A small farming town at the gateway to the Mackenzie Country, famous for its pies.

    Airports: Timaru Airport (TIU) 39 km · Christchurch International Airport (CHC) 153 km

Good to know

  • Driving times are longer than the map suggests; plan fewer stops with more nights.
  • Hire cars and campervans sell out in summer, so book early.
