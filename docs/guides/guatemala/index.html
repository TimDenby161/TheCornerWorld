Places to visit in Guatemala

  • Antigua

    A colonial town of cobbled streets and ruined churches, overlooked by three volcanoes.

    Airport: La Aurora International Airport (GUA) 22 km

  • Lake Atitlan

    A deep highland lake beneath three volcanoes, with Maya villages around the shore linked by boat.

    Airports: La Aurora International Airport (GUA) 70 km · Retalhuleu Airport (RER) 63 km

  • Tikal

    Maya temples rising above the rainforest canopy, with howler monkeys in the trees. Be there for sunrise.

    Airport: Mundo Maya International Airport (FRS) 43 km

  • Acatenango Hike

    A steep overnight climb from Antigua to camp near the summit and watch the neighbouring Fuego volcano erupt after dark.

    Airport: La Aurora International Airport (GUA) 39 km

  • Flores

    A small, colourful town on an island in a lake, and the base for visiting Tikal.

    Airports: Mundo Maya International Airport (FRS) 3 km · La Aurora International Airport (GUA) 270 km

  • Xela

    Quetzaltenango, known to everyone as Xela: a highland city popular for Spanish schools, volcano hikes and hot springs.

    Airports: Retalhuleu Airport (RER) 40 km · La Aurora International Airport (GUA) 111 km
