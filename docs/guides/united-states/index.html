Places to visit in United States

  • New York

    Museums, food, neighbourhoods and skyline. Four nights barely covers it.

    Airport: Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) 14 km

  • New Orleans

    Live music, Creole food and the French Quarter.

    Airport: Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport (MSY) 19 km

  • Grand Canyon

    Stay near the rim for sunrise and sunset.

    Airports: Grand Canyon National Park Airport (GCN) 17 km · Harry Reid International Airport (LAS) 273 km

  • Yellowstone

    Geysers, hot springs, bison and bears in the first national park.

    Airport: Yellowstone Airport (WYS) 51 km

  • Yosemite

    Granite cliffs, waterfalls and giant sequoias.

    Airports: Mammoth Yosemite Airport (MMH) 67 km · Fresno Yosemite International Airport (FAT) 123 km

  • San Francisco

    Hills, the bay and the Golden Gate, and the start of the coast road south.

    Airport: San Francisco International Airport (SFO) 17 km

  • Los Angeles

    Hollywood, Venice Beach and Santa Monica, museums and food from everywhere, spread across a vast sunny sprawl.

    Airports: Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) 20 km · Jack Northrop Field Hawthorne Municipal Airport (HHR) 17 km

  • Las Vegas

    Casinos, shows and neon in the Nevada desert, and a handy base for the Grand Canyon and Death Valley.

    Airport: Harry Reid International Airport (LAS) 10 km

  • Chicago

    Skyscraper architecture on Lake Michigan, blues clubs, big museums and deep-dish pizza.

    Airport: Chicago Midway International Airport (MDW) 15 km

  • Hawaii

    Volcanic islands in the middle of the Pacific, with surf beaches, lava fields and green cliffs. Honolulu is the usual way in.

    Airport: Daniel K. Inouye International Airport (HNL) 7 km

  • Alaska

    Glaciers, bears, whales and the highest mountain in North America, on a scale that needs at least a week. Anchorage is the usual start.

    Airports: Ted Stevens Anchorage International Airport (ANC) 7 km · Merrill Field (MRI) 3 km

  • Death Valley National Park

    The hottest, driest and lowest place in North America: salt flats, sand dunes and painted badlands.

    Airport: Harry Reid International Airport (LAS) 160 km

  • Mount Rushmore

    Four presidents carved into a granite mountain in the Black Hills of South Dakota.

    Airport: Rapid City Regional Airport (RAP) 37 km

  • Philadelphia

    Independence Hall and the Liberty Bell, where the United States began, plus great museums and cheesesteaks.

    Airport: Philadelphia International Airport (PHL) 11 km

  • Dallas

    A big, modern Texan city, with the museum on the Kennedy assassination and a large arts district.

    Airport: Dallas Love Field (DAL) 8 km

  • Fort Worth

    The cowboy side of Texas: the old Stockyards, a daily cattle drive, rodeos and honky-tonks.

    Airports: Dallas Fort Worth International Airport (DFW) 31 km · Fort Worth Meacham International Airport (FTW) 7 km

  • Albuquerque

    An adobe old town on Route 66, a cable car up the Sandia mountains and a hot-air balloon fiesta each October.

    Airport: Albuquerque International Sunport (ABQ) 6 km

  • El Paso

    A desert city in far west Texas on the Rio Grande, facing Mexico, with old Spanish missions nearby.

    Airport: El Paso International Airport (ELP) 12 km

  • Indianapolis

    The home of the Indianapolis 500, with the Speedway and its museum open all year.

    Airport: Indianapolis International Airport (IND) 13 km

  • Rachel

    A speck of a town on Nevada's Extraterrestrial Highway, the closest place to Area 51, with an alien-themed diner and motel.

    Airport: Harry Reid International Airport (LAS) 182 km

Good to know

  • Outside the big cities you will need a car.
