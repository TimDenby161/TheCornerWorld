Places to visit in Brazil

  • Rio de Janeiro

    Christ the Redeemer, Sugarloaf Mountain and the beaches of Copacabana and Ipanema, in a city squeezed between mountains and sea.

    Airport: Santos Dumont Airport (SDU) 1 km

    Hundreds of waterfalls in the rainforest on the border with Argentina. See both sides: Brazil for the view, Argentina for the walkways.

    Airport: Cataratas International Airport (IGU) 12 km
