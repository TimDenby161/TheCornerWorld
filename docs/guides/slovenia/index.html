Places to visit in Slovenia

  • Ljubljana

    A small, leafy capital with a castle on the hill and cafes all along the river.

    Airport: Ljubljana Jože Pučnik Airport (LJU) 20 km

  • Lake Bled

    An alpine lake with a church on an island and a castle on the cliff above.

    Airport: Ljubljana Jože Pučnik Airport (LJU) 32 km

  • Vintgar Gorge

    A boardwalk through a narrow gorge above a clear green river, a few minutes from Bled.

    Airport: Ljubljana Jože Pučnik Airport (LJU) 34 km
