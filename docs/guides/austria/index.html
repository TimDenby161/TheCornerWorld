Places to visit in Austria

  • Innsbruck

    A painted old town with mountains at the end of every street, and a cable car straight up from the centre.

    Airport: Innsbruck Airport (INN) 4 km

  • Vienna

    Imperial palaces, grand coffee houses and a concert or opera most nights of the year.

    Airport: Vienna International Airport (VIE) 19 km

  • Salzburg

    Mozart's birthplace: a baroque old town under a hilltop fortress, with lakes and mountains close by.

    Airport: Salzburg Airport (SZG) 3 km
