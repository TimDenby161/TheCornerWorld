Places to visit in Ecuador

  • Galapagos

    Volcanic islands far out in the Pacific, where giant tortoises, sea lions and iguanas have no fear of people. Seen by cruise or island-hopping.

    Airport: Seymour Galapagos Ecological Airport (GPS) 32 km

  • Quito

    A capital high in the Andes with a large colonial old town, almost on the equator.

    Airport: Mariscal Sucre International Airport (UIO) 14 km
