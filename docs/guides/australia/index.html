Places to visit in Australia

  • Sydney

    The Opera House and Harbour Bridge, ferries across the harbour and surf beaches from Bondi to Manly.

    Airport: Sydney Kingsford Smith International Airport (SYD) 9 km

  • Great Barrier Reef

    The world's largest coral reef, for snorkelling, diving and sailing. Boats go from the Whitsundays, Cairns and Port Douglas.

    Airport: Hamilton Island Airport (HTI) 26 km

  • Uluru

    The great red rock at the centre of the continent, sacred to the Anangu, best at sunrise and sunset.

    Airport: Ayers Rock Connellan Airport (AYQ) 18 km

  • Melbourne

    Laneway cafes and bars, street art, big sporting events and the country's best food scene.

    Airports: Melbourne Airport (MEL) 19 km · Melbourne Essendon Airport (MEB) 10 km

  • Great Ocean Road

    A coastal drive west of Melbourne past surf beaches and rainforest to the sea stacks of the Twelve Apostles.

    Airport: Melbourne Avalon International Airport (AVV) 106 km

  • K'gari

    The world's largest sand island, formerly Fraser Island: freshwater lakes, rainforest and a beach you drive along by 4x4.

    Airports: Hervey Bay Airport (HVB) 27 km · Sunshine Coast Airport (MCY) 151 km

  • Cairns

    A tropical city in the far north, between the reef and the Daintree Rainforest.

    Airport: Cairns International Airport (CNS) 5 km

  • Brisbane

    A sunny river city with a man-made beach on the South Bank and islands in the bay.

    Airport: Brisbane International Airport (BNE) 13 km

  • Byron Bay

    A laid-back surf town at Australia's most easterly point, with a lighthouse walk and whales offshore in winter.

    Airports: Gold Coast Airport (OOL) 54 km · Ballina Byron Gateway Airport (BNK) 22 km

  • Blue Mountains

    Sandstone cliffs, eucalyptus valleys and the Three Sisters, two hours by train from Sydney.

    Airports: Sydney Kingsford Smith International Airport (SYD) 84 km · Bathurst Airport (BHS) 70 km

  • Hobart

    Tasmania's harbour capital, with the Salamanca market, the MONA art museum and a mountain looming behind.

    Airport: Hobart International Airport (HBA) 16 km

  • Cradle Mountain

    A jagged peak above a glacial lake in the Tasmanian wilderness, with wombats on the walking tracks.

    Airports: Devonport Airport (DPO) 67 km · Hobart International Airport (HBA) 184 km

  • Bay of Fires

    White beaches and orange-lichened granite boulders on Tasmania's north-east coast.

    Airports: Launceston Airport (LST) 98 km · Hobart International Airport (HBA) 196 km

  • Kangaroo Island

    Sea lions, koalas and kangaroos in the wild, and a rugged coast, a ferry ride from the mainland near Adelaide.

    Airports: Kingscote Airport (KGC) 29 km · Adelaide International Airport (ADL) 152 km

  • Perth

    A sunny, isolated city on the Indian Ocean, with Fremantle's port and the quokkas of Rottnest Island.

    Airport: Perth International Airport (PER) 10 km

  • Magnetic Island

    A hilly island of bays and boulders off Townsville, with wild koalas along the walking tracks.

    Airports: Townsville Airport / RAAF Base Townsville (TSV) 15 km · Cairns International Airport (CNS) 277 km

  • Darwin

    The tropical capital of the Top End, and the way in to Kakadu and Litchfield national parks.

    Airport: Darwin International Airport / RAAF Darwin (DRW) 7 km

  • Adelaide

    A relaxed, easy city with a great food market, beaches and the Barossa wine region an hour away.

    Airport: Adelaide International Airport (ADL) 6 km

  • Gold Coast

    A long strip of surf beaches backed by high-rises, with theme parks and rainforest inland.

    Airport: Gold Coast Airport (OOL) 20 km

  • Alice Springs

    The outback town in the Red Centre, among the gorges of the MacDonnell Ranges.

    Airport: Alice Springs Airport (ASP) 12 km

  • Rainbow Beach

    A small beach town under cliffs of coloured sand, and a jumping-off point for K'gari.

    Airports: Sunshine Coast Airport (MCY) 77 km · Hervey Bay Airport (HVB) 68 km

  • Townsville

    A dry-tropics city with a seafront promenade and the ferry to Magnetic Island.

    Airports: Townsville Airport / RAAF Base Townsville (TSV) 6 km · Cairns International Airport (CNS) 288 km

  • Mornington Peninsula

    Bay beaches, wineries and hot springs, an hour south of Melbourne.

    Airports: Melbourne Avalon International Airport (AVV) 60 km · Melbourne Moorabbin Airport (MBW) 42 km

  • Coffs Harbour

    A beach city halfway between Sydney and Brisbane, with a harbour walk and the Big Banana.

    Airports: Coffs Harbour Airport (CFS) 2 km · Gold Coast Airport (OOL) 240 km

  • Woolgoolga

    A small surf town north of Coffs Harbour, with a headland for whale watching and a large Sikh temple.

    Airports: Coffs Harbour Airport (CFS) 25 km · Gold Coast Airport (OOL) 218 km

  • Canberra

    The planned capital, with the national museums, galleries and Parliament House.

    Airports: Canberra Airport (CBR) 7 km · Sydney Kingsford Smith International Airport (SYD) 239 km
