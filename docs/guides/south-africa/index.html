Places to visit in South Africa

  • Cape Town

    Table Mountain above the sea, penguins at Boulders Beach, the Cape of Good Hope and vineyards an hour away.

    Airport: Cape Town International Airport (CPT) 18 km

  • Kruger National Park

    A park the size of a small country, where you can drive yourself in search of the Big Five.

    Airports: Skukuza Airport (SZK) 3 km · Kruger Mpumalanga International Airport (MQP) 66 km

  • Johannesburg

    The country's biggest city, with the Apartheid Museum, Soweto and the main international airport.

    Airport: O.R. Tambo International Airport (JNB) 21 km
