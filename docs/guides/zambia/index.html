Places to visit in Zambia

  • South Luangwa National Park

    One of Africa's great parks, known for leopards, huge hippo pools and safaris on foot. Mfuwe is the base.

    Airport: Mfuwe International Airport (MFU) 24 km

  • Lusaka

    The capital and transport hub, with busy markets and the main airport.

    Airport: Kenneth Kaunda International Airport (LUN) 21 km

  • Chipata

    A border town near Malawi, and the last stop for supplies on the way to South Luangwa.

    Airport: Mfuwe International Airport (MFU) 88 km
