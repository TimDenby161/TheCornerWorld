Places to visit in Greece

  • Athens

    The Acropolis above a busy modern city, with some of the world's great ancient museums.

    Airport: Athens Eleftherios Venizelos International Airport (ATH) 19 km

  • Crete

    The largest Greek island: Minoan palaces, gorges and beaches. Big enough to need a car and most of a week.

    Airport: Heraklion International Nikos Kazantzakis Airport (HER) 5 km

  • Corfu

    A green Ionian island with a Venetian old town and coves along the west coast.

    Airport: Corfu Ioannis Kapodistrias International Airport (CFU) 2 km

  • Rhodes

    A medieval walled city built by the Knights, with beaches and the acropolis of Lindos down the coast.

    Airport: Rhodes International Airport "Diagoras" (RHO) 13 km

  • Mykonos

    Whitewashed lanes, windmills and beach clubs, with the ruins of Delos a short boat ride away.

    Airports: Mykonos Island National Airport (JMK) 2 km · Santorini International Airport (JTR) 117 km
