Places to visit in Portugal

  • Lisbon

    Hills, trams and tiled streets above the Tagus, with beaches a short train ride away.

    Airport: Lisbon Humberto Delgado Airport (LIS) 7 km

  • Porto

    Steep streets down to the Douro, port cellars across the river and the valley's vineyards beyond.

    Airport: Francisco de Sá Carneiro Airport (OPO) 12 km

  • Coimbra

    A hilltop university town between Lisbon and Porto, with a great baroque library.

    Airports: Aerodromo Goncalves Lobato (Viseu Airport) (VSE) 73 km · Francisco de Sá Carneiro Airport (OPO) 117 km
