Places to visit in Switzerland

  • Zurich

    A lakeside old town with good museums, and the easiest place to arrive.

    Airport: Zürich Airport (ZRH) 10 km

  • Kandersteg

    A mountain village in the Bernese Oberland, with the blue Oeschinen Lake a short walk above it.

    Airports: Bern Airport (BRN) 49 km · Geneva International Airport (GVA) 123 km

  • Lugano

    Italian-speaking Switzerland: a palm-lined lake, piazzas and mountains behind.

    Airports: Lugano Airport (LUG) 3 km · Zürich Airport (ZRH) 165 km
