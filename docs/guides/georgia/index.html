Places to visit in Georgia

  • Tbilisi

    Carved wooden balconies, sulphur baths and a fortress above the river, with food and wine to match.

    Airport: Tbilisi International Airport (TBS) 15 km

  • Sighnaghi

    A walled hilltop town above the vineyards of Kakheti, and the base for visiting wineries.

    Airport: Tbilisi International Airport (TBS) 80 km

  • Gudauri

    A ski resort high in the Greater Caucasus on the Georgian Military Highway, with hiking and paragliding in summer.

    Airport: Tbilisi International Airport (TBS) 98 km

  • Batumi

    A subtropical Black Sea resort with a long seafront boulevard and showy modern towers.

    Airport: Alexander Kartveli Batumi International Airport (BUS) 6 km
