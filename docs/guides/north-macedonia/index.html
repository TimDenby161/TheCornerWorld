Places to visit in North Macedonia

  • Lake Ohrid

    One of Europe's oldest and deepest lakes, with an old town of churches on its shore.

    Airport: Ohrid St. Paul the Apostle Airport (OHD) 9 km

  • Skopje

    An Ottoman bazaar on one bank and a centre crowded with new statues on the other.

    Airport: Skopje International Airport (SKP) 17 km

  • Matka Canyon

    A steep-sided gorge and lake just outside Skopje, for kayaking, caves and cliffside monasteries.

    Airport: Skopje International Airport (SKP) 27 km
