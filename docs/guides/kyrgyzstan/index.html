Places to visit in Kyrgyzstan

  • Issyk Kul

    A huge mountain lake that never freezes, with beaches on the north shore and snowy peaks all round.

    Airport: Issyk-Kul International Airport (IKU) 32 km

  • Song-Kul

    A high lake among summer pastures, where you stay in a yurt with herding families. Reachable June to September.

    Airport: Manas International Airport (BSZ) 146 km

  • Karakol

    A small town at the eastern end of Issyk Kul, and the base for trekking in the Tien Shan.

    Airports: Karakol International Airport (IKG) 2 km · Issyk-Kul International Airport (IKU) 139 km

  • Bishkek

    A green, Soviet-planned capital with mountains on the horizon and a big bazaar.

    Airport: Manas International Airport (BSZ) 23 km
