Places to visit in China

  • Beijing

    The Forbidden City, the Temple of Heaven and day trips out to the Great Wall.

    Airport: Beijing Capital International Airport (PEK) 25 km

  • Forbidden City

    The emperors' palace in the middle of Beijing, with almost a thousand buildings behind red walls. Book ahead.

    Airport: Beijing Capital International Airport (PEK) 24 km

  • Mutianyu Great Wall

    A restored stretch of the Great Wall over wooded hills, quieter than Badaling and a day trip from Beijing.

    Airport: Beijing Capital International Airport (PEK) 39 km

  • Shanghai

    The Bund's old waterfront facing the towers of Pudong, in China's biggest and most modern city.

    Airport: Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport (SHA) 13 km

  • Xi'an

    The old capital at the start of the Silk Road, with complete city walls and a busy Muslim Quarter.

    Airport: Xi'an Xianyang International Airport (XIY) 26 km

  • Terracotta Warriors

    Thousands of life-size clay soldiers guarding the first emperor's tomb, just outside Xi'an.

    Airport: Xi'an Xianyang International Airport (XIY) 48 km

  • Chengdu

    The home of giant pandas, teahouses and fiery Sichuan food.

    Airport: Chengdu Shuangliu International Airport (CTU) 16 km

  • Zhangjiajie

    A forest of sandstone pillars rising out of the mist, with glass bridges and cliff walkways.

    Airport: Zhangjiajie Hehua International Airport (DYG) 25 km

  • Yangshuo

    Limestone peaks along the Li River, explored by bamboo raft and bicycle.

    Airport: Guilin Liangjiang International Airport (KWL) 67 km

  • Lhasa

    The Potala Palace and the Jokhang Temple, high on the Tibetan plateau. Foreign visitors need a permit and an organised tour.

    Airport: Lhasa Gonggar International Airport (LXA) 45 km

  • Jiuzhaigou Valley

    Turquoise lakes, waterfalls and forest in a high valley in northern Sichuan.

    Airport: Jiuzhai Huanglong Airport (JZH) 50 km

  • Suzhou

    Classical gardens and canals, half an hour from Shanghai by fast train.

    Airport: Sunan Shuofang International Airport (WUX) 28 km

  • Leshan Giant Buddha

    A seated Buddha 71 metres tall, carved into a river cliff. A day trip from Chengdu.

    Airports: Leshan Airport (LSG) 11 km · Chengdu Tianfu International Airport (TFU) 108 km

  • Chongqing

    A vast city stacked on hills above the Yangtze, famous for its hotpot and its night-time skyline.

    Airport: Chongqing Jiangbei International Airport (CKG) 20 km

  • Harbin

    A far-northern city with Russian architecture and a huge ice and snow festival in January and February.

    Airport: Harbin Taiping International Airport (HRB) 29 km

  • Sanya

    China's tropical beach resort, at the southern tip of Hainan island.

    Airport: Sanya Phoenix International Airport (SYX) 12 km

  • Hainan

    A tropical island off the south coast, with beaches, rainforest and volcanic hot springs. Haikou is the capital.

    Airport: Haikou Meilan International Airport (HAK) 17 km

  • Dandong

    A border city on the Yalu River, looking straight across to North Korea.

    Airports: Dandong Langtou International Airport (DDG) 14 km · Shenyang Taoxian International Airport (SHE) 185 km

  • Urumqi

    The capital of Xinjiang in the far west, with bazaars and the mountain lake of Tianchi nearby.

    Airport: Ürümqi Tianshan International Airport (URC) 15 km

  • Shijiazhuang Table Tennis Centre

    The national table tennis training base at Zhengding, just outside Shijiazhuang, where China's champions train.

    Airport: Shijiazhuang Zhengding International Airport (SJW) 18 km
