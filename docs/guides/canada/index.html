Places to visit in Canada

  • Banff National Park

    Turquoise lakes, glaciers and peaks in the Rockies, with a lively small town at the centre.

    Airport: Calgary International Airport (YYC) 109 km

  • Lake Louise

    A glacier-fed lake of milky blue under a wall of mountains, with Moraine Lake just up the road.

    Airport: Calgary International Airport (YYC) 155 km

  • Vancouver

    A city between ocean and mountains, with Stanley Park, good food and ski slopes in sight of downtown.

    Airport: Vancouver International Airport (YVR) 11 km

  • Niagara Falls

    Three huge waterfalls on the border with the United States, best seen from the Canadian side and from the boat at their foot.

    Airports: Niagara District Airport (YCM) 14 km · John C. Munro Hamilton International Airport (YHM) 70 km

  • Toronto

    Canada's biggest city, on Lake Ontario, with the CN Tower and some of the most varied neighbourhoods and food anywhere.

    Airports: Toronto Pearson International Airport (YYZ) 20 km · Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport (YTZ) 3 km

  • Churchill

    A remote town on Hudson Bay, reached only by train or plane, for polar bears in autumn and beluga whales in summer.

    Airport: Churchill Airport (YYQ) 7 km
