Places to visit in Cambodia

  • Siem Reap

    The town beside the temples of Angkor. Three days covers Angkor Wat, the Bayon and the jungle-covered Ta Prohm.

    Airport: Siem Reap-Angkor International Airport (SAI) 39 km

  • Phnom Penh

    A riverside capital with the Royal Palace, busy markets and the sobering memorials of the Khmer Rouge years.

    Airport: Techo International Airport (KTI) 22 km

  • Koh Rong

    An island of white-sand beaches and jungle, a ferry ride from Sihanoukville.

    Airport: Dara Sakor International Airport (DSY) 22 km

  • Kampot

    A sleepy river town of old shophouses, known for its pepper farms and the hill station of Bokor.

    Airport: Sihanouk International Airport (KOS) 60 km

  • Battambang

    A quiet city of colonial streets, with countryside temples and the bamboo train nearby.

    Airports: Battambang Airport (BBM) 3 km · Siem Reap-Angkor International Airport (SAI) 115 km
