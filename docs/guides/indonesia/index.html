Places to visit in Indonesia

  • Yogyakarta

    Java's cultural capital, and the base for the great temples of Borobudur and Prambanan.

    Airports: Yogyakarta International Airport (YIA) 35 km · Adisutjipto International Airport (JOG) 8 km

  • Komodo Island

    The home of the Komodo dragon, with pink beaches and superb diving. Boats go from Labuan Bajo on Flores.

    Airport: Sultan Muhammad Salahuddin Airport (BMU) 89 km

  • Jakarta

    The vast, crowded capital on Java, where most international flights arrive.

    Airport: Halim Perdanakusuma International Airport (HLP) 8 km
