Places to visit in Laos

  • Luang Prabang

    A former royal capital of gilded temples and French-era streets on the Mekong, with the Kuang Si waterfalls nearby.

    Airport: Luang Phabang International Airport (LPQ) 4 km

  • Vang Vieng

    A riverside town among limestone cliffs, for caves, blue lagoons, kayaking and hot-air balloons.

    Airport: Wattay International Airport (VTE) 105 km

  • Nong Khiaw

    A quiet village on the Nam Ou river, hemmed in by huge karst mountains, with viewpoint hikes and boat trips.

    Airport: Luang Phabang International Airport (LPQ) 87 km

  • Vientiane

    One of the world's sleepiest capitals, with golden stupas and sunset on the Mekong.

    Airport: Wattay International Airport (VTE) 4 km

  • Houayxay

    The border town opposite Thailand where the two-day slow boat down the Mekong begins.

    Airports: Bokeo International Airport (BOR) 26 km · Luang Phabang International Airport (LPQ) 188 km

  • Pak Beng

    A small riverside village, and the overnight stop on the slow boat to Luang Prabang.

    Airport: Luang Phabang International Airport (LPQ) 108 km
