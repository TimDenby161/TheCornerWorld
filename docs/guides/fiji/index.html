Places to visit in Fiji

  • Yasawa Islands

    A chain of volcanic islands with empty beaches and blue lagoons, reached by a daily catamaran from Port Denarau.

    Airport: Nadi International Airport (NAN) 91 km

  • Nadi

    Where international flights arrive, and the jumping-off point for the islands from nearby Port Denarau.

    Airport: Nadi International Airport (NAN) 5 km

  • South Sea Island

    A tiny coral island in the Mamanucas you can walk round in minutes, half an hour by boat from Port Denarau.

    Airport: Nadi International Airport (NAN) 23 km

  • Suva

    The capital, on the wetter side of the main island, with a big produce market, colonial buildings and the Fiji Museum.

    Airport: Nausori International Airport (SUV) 17 km
