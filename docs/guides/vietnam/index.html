South-Eastern Asia · Capital Hanoi

A long, thin country best travelled north to south or the reverse: limestone bays, old trading towns, big energetic cities and some of the best street food in Asia.

Places to visit in Vietnam

  • Hanoi

    The Old Quarter, lakes and street food. A good place to start or finish.

    Airport: Noi Bai International Airport (HAN) 22 km

  • Ha Long Bay

    Thousands of limestone islands, usually seen on an overnight boat.

    Airports: Cat Bi International Airport (HPH) 48 km · Van Don International Airport (VDO) 34 km

  • Ninh Binh

    Karst peaks rising out of rice paddies, explored by rowing boat and bicycle.

    Airports: Noi Bai International Airport (HAN) 109 km · Cat Bi International Airport (HPH) 101 km

  • Hue

    The old imperial capital, with its citadel and royal tombs.

    Airport: Phu Bai International Airport (HUI) 14 km

  • Hoi An

    A lantern-lit old trading port known for tailors, food and nearby beaches.

    Airport: Da Nang International Airport (DAD) 24 km

  • Ho Chi Minh City

    The fast-moving southern hub, and the gateway to the Mekong Delta.

    Airport: Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN) 2 km

  • Ha Giang

    The far-northern mountains, best seen on a three or four day motorbike loop over high passes.

    Airport: Noi Bai International Airport (HAN) 197 km

  • Sa Pa

    A hill town above terraced rice valleys, for trekking between minority villages.

    Airports: Noi Bai International Airport (HAN) 238 km · Dien Bien Phu Airport (DIN) 135 km

  • Cat Ba

    A rugged island on the edge of Ha Long Bay, and the base for quieter Lan Ha Bay.

    Airport: Cat Bi International Airport (HPH) 35 km

  • Da Nang

    A modern beach city with the main airport of central Vietnam, between Hue and Hoi An.

    Airport: Da Nang International Airport (DAD) 1 km

  • Da Lat

    A cool hill town of pine forest, waterfalls, flower farms and coffee.

    Airports: Lien Khuong Airport (DLI) 23 km · Cam Ranh International Airport / Cam Ranh Air Base (CXR) 85 km

  • Phu Quoc

    A large island in the far south, for white-sand beaches and a few lazy days at the end of a trip.

    Airport: Phú Quốc International Airport (PQC) 7 km

  • Nha Trang

    A busy beach resort city on a long bay, with islands and diving offshore.

    Airport: Cam Ranh International Airport / Cam Ranh Air Base (CXR) 27 km

  • Mui Ne

    A beach strip known for kitesurfing, with red and white sand dunes just inland.

    Airports: Lien Khuong Airport (DLI) 92 km · Cam Ranh International Airport / Cam Ranh Air Base (CXR) 156 km
