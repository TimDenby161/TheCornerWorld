Places to visit in Jordan

  • Amman

    The capital: a Roman theatre, the hilltop Citadel and the best food in the country.

    Airports: Queen Alia International Airport (AMM) 26 km · Marka International (Amman Civil) Airport (ADJ) 6 km

  • Petra

    The Nabataean city carved into rose-coloured cliffs. Give it two days to reach the Monastery.

    Airport: King Hussein International Airport (AQJ) 90 km

  • Wadi Rum

    Sandstone mountains and red dunes. Stay a night in a desert camp.

    Airport: King Hussein International Airport (AQJ) 39 km

  • Dead Sea

    Float in the salt water at the lowest point on Earth, then do nothing for a day.

    Airport: Queen Alia International Airport (AMM) 53 km

Good to know

  • Hiring a car is the simplest way to link Amman, Petra, Wadi Rum and the Dead Sea.
  • Start Petra at opening time to see the Treasury before the crowds.
