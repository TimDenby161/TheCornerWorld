Places to visit in Botswana

  • Okavango Delta

    A river that spreads into the Kalahari and never reaches the sea, explored by dugout canoe. Trips start from Maun.

    Airport: Maun International Airport (MUB) 2 km

  • Chobe National Park

    The largest elephant herds in Africa, best seen from a boat on the Chobe River. Kasane is the base.

    Airport: Kasane International Airport (BBK) 4 km
