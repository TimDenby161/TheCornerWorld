Places to visit in Malaysia

  • Kuala Lumpur

    The Petronas Towers, the Batu Caves and some of Asia's best street food.

    Airport: Sultan Abdul Aziz Shah International Airport (SZB) 16 km

  • Penang

    The old streets of George Town, with street art, clan houses and the best hawker food in Malaysia.

    Airport: Penang International Airport (PEN) 14 km

  • Langkawi

    A duty-free island of beaches, jungle and a cable car up to a bridge in the sky.

    Airport: Langkawi International Airport (LGK) 8 km
