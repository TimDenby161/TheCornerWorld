Places to visit in England

  • London

    Museums, theatre, markets and royal parks. Four nights covers the main sights without rushing.

    Airports: London Heathrow Airport (LHR) 23 km · London City Airport (LCY) 13 km

  • Lake District

    Lakes, fells and stone villages: England's best walking country.

    Airports: Newcastle International Airport (NCL) 110 km · Teesside International Airport (MME) 107 km

  • Cornwall

    Surf beaches, fishing harbours and the coast path, at the far south-west tip.

    Airports: Cornwall Airport Newquay (NQY) 17 km · Cardiff International Airport (CWL) 164 km

  • Devon

    Two coasts, Dartmoor in between, and cream teas everywhere.

    Airports: Exeter International Airport (EXT) 27 km · Cardiff International Airport (CWL) 84 km

  • Manchester

    Music, football and industrial history in the big city of the north-west.

    Airport: Manchester Airport (MAN) 15 km

  • Liverpool

    The Beatles, the waterfront docks and two football clubs.

    Airport: Liverpool John Lennon Airport (LPL) 13 km

  • Seven Sisters

    A line of white chalk cliffs on the Sussex coast, with a clifftop walk along the top.

    Airport: London Gatwick Airport (LGW) 50 km

  • Bath

    Roman baths and honey-coloured Georgian crescents, in a city small enough to walk.

    Airport: Bristol Airport (BRS) 25 km

  • Oxford

    Old colleges, quiet quads and riverside pubs, an hour from London.

    Airport: London Luton Airport (LTN) 63 km

  • Brighton

    A pebble beach, a pier and the narrow shopping streets of the Lanes, in the liveliest town on the south coast.

    Airport: London Gatwick Airport (LGW) 37 km

  • Peak District

    Gritstone edges, limestone dales and stone villages, between Manchester and Sheffield.

    Airport: Manchester Airport (MAN) 32 km

  • Norfolk

    Wide sandy beaches, seals and salt marsh on the north coast, with the Broads inland.

    Airports: Norwich Airport (NWI) 42 km · London Stansted Airport (STN) 126 km

  • Mablethorpe

    A traditional seaside town on the Lincolnshire coast, with a long sandy beach and dunes.

    Airports: Humberside Airport (HUY) 48 km · East Midlands Airport (EMA) 120 km
