Privacy

You can use the map, the guides and the trip planner without telling us anything: a trip you build stays in your own browser. This page is about what happens if you ask us to plan a trip, pay us, or join our emails.

If you ask us to plan a trip

  • Your brief: the form on our planning page opens your own email app, so what you send reaches us as an email: your name, your email address and the details of your trip. We keep it, and our emails with you, to plan your trip and make the changes you ask for.
  • Your trip page: we publish it on this site locked with a password that only you are sent. Without the password it can't be read, even by someone who finds the file.
  • Paying: payments are handled by Stripe. We see your name, email address and what you paid, never your card details.

We keep briefs and trip pages until you ask us to remove them, and payment records for six years, as UK tax law requires.

If you join our emails

Our mailing list is kept by Kit (kit.com), whose servers are in the United States. We hold your email address (and your name, if you give it) and use it only to send you our emails. Every email has a link to unsubscribe, which removes you at once.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, or for it to be corrected or deleted, by emailing timdenby161@gmail.com. If you are unhappy with how we have handled it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
