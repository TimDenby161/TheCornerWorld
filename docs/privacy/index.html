Privacy

You can use the map without telling us anything. This page is about what happens if you ask us to plan a trip, pay us, or join our emails.

Last updated 17 November 2026.

Who we are

The Corner World is run by Tim Denby, a sole trader in the UK, who decides how your information is used (the "controller", in the words of UK data protection law). To ask anything about your information, email timdenby161@gmail.com, or write to Tim Denby, Flat 127, 21 Durnsford Road, London SW19 8GW.

If you ask us to plan a trip

  • Your brief: the form on our planning page opens your own email app, so what you send reaches us as an email: your name, your email address and the details of your trip, which may include who is travelling (and children's ages) and anything you tell us about mobility or health. We keep it, and our emails with you, in our Gmail account, to plan your trip and make the changes you ask for. Please only tell us about health or mobility what we need to plan around.
  • Your trip page: we publish it on this site locked with a password that only you are sent. Without the password it can't be read, even by someone who finds the file. Its address doesn't include your name.
  • Paying: payments are handled by Stripe. We see your name, email address and what you paid, never your card details.

Why we may use it: to agree and carry out our contract with you (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Anything you tell us about health or mobility is used only with your consent, which you give by sending it and can withdraw at any time. Payment records we keep because tax law requires it (Article 6(1)(c)).

How long: your brief, our emails and your trip page until two years after we deliver the page, so we can answer questions about the trip, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Payment records for six years, as UK tax law requires.

If you join our emails

Our mailing list is kept by Kit (kit.com). We hold your email address (and your name, if you give it) and use it only to send you our emails, including the launch offer code if you asked for it. We do this because you agreed to it (Article 6(1)(a)). Every email has a link to unsubscribe, which removes you at once, and we keep your address until you do.

When you visit the site

  • No tracking: the site has no analytics, no advertising and no cookies of its own, and loads nothing from other websites.
  • Hosting: the site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records the internet (IP) address of each visit in its logs, to keep the service running and secure. We can't see these logs.
  • Your browser: your choice of light or dark, and, on a trip page, the changes you make and the page's password (so you aren't asked again) are kept in your own browser's storage. They are never sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.
  • Links: links to other sites, such as GOV.UK or booking sites, take you to sites with their own privacy notices.

Who else handles it

Only the companies that run the services above for us: Google (Gmail), Stripe (payments), Kit (our emails) and GitHub (hosting). Each may only use it to provide that service. Some of them hold it in the United States; where they do, it is protected by the UK's data bridge with the US (the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or by the standard data protection contracts approved for the UK. We never sell your information or share it for advertising.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, or for it to be corrected or deleted, ask us to stop or limit using it, or object to how we use it, and you can withdraw any consent you have given, by emailing timdenby161@gmail.com. We will reply within a month. If you are unhappy with how we have handled it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and the date at the top. If you are a client, we'll tell you about a change that affects you by email.
