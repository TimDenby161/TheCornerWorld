What you're buying

A trip page: a web page made for your trip, with the route, every day hour by hour, an estimate of every cost in pounds for your group, and a list of what to book. It's a plan only. We don't book or sell any travel (see "What we don't do" below).

  • Short trip, £79: up to 7 days and up to 3 stops, with 1 round of changes.
  • Standard, £149: 8 to 16 days and up to 7 stops in one or two countries, with flights and the order of stops worked out, entry and visa notes for UK passports, and 2 rounds of changes.
  • Big trip, £229: 17 days or more, or 3 or more countries, with as many stops as the trip needs, another way to do the route with the cost of each, and 3 rounds of changes.

Prices are in pounds and are for the whole page, whatever the size of your group.

How it works

  • Your brief: you send us the form on our planning page. We reply within one working day with any questions, the plan that fits and a link to pay.
  • Our contract: it starts when you pay. Nothing is charged before then.
  • Delivery: we send you your trip page within 5 working days of your payment, or of your answers to our questions if those come later. If we can't, we'll tell you why and when it will be ready, and you can cancel for a full refund.
  • Changes: a round of changes is one list of changes sent in one email, such as "more beach, one stop fewer". We make each round within 3 working days. Ask for them within 6 months of delivery and before your trip starts. Changes that turn it into a different trip (a new country, or many more days) may need a different plan: we'll agree that with you before doing anything.
  • Places we won't plan: if the UK government advises against all travel, or all but essential travel, to somewhere in your brief, we'll tell you before you pay, and won't plan travel there.

Your right to cancel

You have 14 days from the day you pay to cancel, without giving a reason. To cancel, tell us clearly by email; you can use the form at the end of these terms, but you don't have to.

  • If we haven't started: we refund you in full.
  • If you asked us to start straight away and cancel before your page is delivered, we refund what you paid less a fair amount for the work we've done by the time you tell us.
  • Once your page is delivered, if you asked us to start within the 14 days and agreed that you'd lose the right to cancel once it was delivered, you can no longer cancel. We ask you to confirm both when we send the payment link, because we can only start work early if you want us to.

We refund you within 14 days of your cancelling, to the card you paid with.

If you're not happy

The rounds of changes are there to get it right. If, after you've used them, your page still doesn't give you what we agreed, tell us and we'll refund you in full. We must plan your trip with reasonable care and skill: nothing here takes away your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

What we don't do

  • We don't book or sell travel. You book flights, hotels, transport and tours directly, and your contract for each is with that company. Your trip isn't a package holiday and isn't ATOL protected: you keep the protection of the company and the card you pay with.
  • We don't promise prices or timetables. We research each one when we plan, and mark anything we couldn't confirm. Prices, timetables, opening hours and entry rules change: check them, especially visas and passport rules, before you book or travel.
  • We don't replace official advice or insurance. Read the UK government's travel advice for every country you visit, get travel insurance, and check health advice and vaccinations (travelhealthpro.org.uk).
