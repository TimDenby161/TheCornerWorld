Questions

Good to know

What don't you do?

We don't take bookings or your money for travel: you book directly, so you keep the protection of the airline, hotel or card you pay with. Prices are careful estimates, not promises, and we don't replace travel insurance or GOV.UK travel advice.

Can I change the page myself?

Yes. You can add activities to free time, move or remove them, switch hotel prices and mark things booked or paid. The page saves your changes on your device. For bigger changes, like a new stop or different dates, ask us and we'll update it.

Where can I go?

Almost anywhere: the map scores over 200 countries, and we can plan a trip to any of them. If somewhere is hard to reach or unsafe, we'll tell you before you pay.

Does it work without signal?

Once the page has opened on your phone it keeps your changes there. For remote areas, save a copy or take screenshots of each day before you go.

How accurate are the prices?

We research each one at the time we plan. Hotel and flight prices move, so treat them as a careful estimate and book the important things early. The booking list puts them in order.

Are the links ads?

Some booking links on the page may earn us a small commission at no cost to you. We only link to places we'd choose ourselves, and you're free to book anywhere.

What if I don't like it?

The rounds of changes are there to get it right. If we can't make it work for you after them, we'll refund you.
