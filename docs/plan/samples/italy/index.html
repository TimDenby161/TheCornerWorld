    Two one-way flights, in to Florence and home from Naples, so there's no going back up the country. A 4-year-old needs their own seat and usually pays about the adult fare.

    Book now

    1. Flights, about £540 for 3 with cabin bags: Gatwick → Florence Sat 22 May (search), Naples → Gatwick Mon 31 May (search)
    2. Hotels with a family room (ask for a real bed, not a cot, for a 4-year-old): Sorrento first, as late May is busy
    3. Timed tickets: Uffizi for Sun 23 May, Colosseum for Wed 26 May, Vatican Museums for Thu 27 May
    4. Trains on trenitalia.com, cheaper well ahead: Florence → Rome Tue 25 May, Rome → Naples Fri 28 May. Children 4–14 pay half
    5. Campania Express seats from Naples to Sorrento, Fri 28 May: it runs only a few times a day
    6. Travel insurance for all three of you

    Check closer to the time

    • Read GOV.UK travel advice for Italy the week before you go
    • ETIAS: the EU's €20 travel authorisation for UK adults (children free) was due to start in late 2026. Check whether you need it
    • Passports: issued under 10 years before the day you arrive, and valid 3 months after you leave
    • Flight times for May 2027: these are from the 2026 timetable
    • City tax is paid at each hotel, per adult per night. Children are exempt
    • Capri ferries on the day: they stop when the sea is rough
