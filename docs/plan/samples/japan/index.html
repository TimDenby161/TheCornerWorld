    There's no direct flight from Osaka to London. Flying in to Tokyo and home from Osaka on one booking saves a day of going back to Tokyo. Price it against a return to Tokyo before you book.

    Book now

    1. Flights on one booking, about £720 with one stop each way: Heathrow → Tokyo Haneda Sat 15 May (search), Osaka Kansai → Heathrow Sat 29 May (search)
    2. Sumo tickets for Tue 18 May: on sale 10 April 2027 on the Japan Sumo Association's ticket site, and good seats go within hours
    3. Timed tickets: teamLab Planets and the Shibuya Sky sunset slot, Mon 17 May
    4. Hakone ryokan with dinner, Thu 20 May: many charge solo guests for two, so look for single rates (about £120)
    5. Business hotels (Toyoko Inn, APA, Dormy Inn and the like, about £55 a night), Kyoto first: May is busy
    6. Travel insurance

    Check closer to the time

    • Read GOV.UK travel advice for Japan the week before you go
    • UK passports: 90 days visa-free. Japan plans a pre-travel authorisation (JESTA) from about 2028: check it isn't needed yet
    • Flight times for May 2027: these are from the 2026 timetable
    • Bullet trains: buy single tickets on smartEX a few days ahead. A 7-day JR Pass (¥50,000) costs more than this route's tickets
