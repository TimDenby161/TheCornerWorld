## Thatch: services

Thatch sells "itinerary" services. Create one for each plan, or one service with a price for each day if Thatch requires it. Its pricing options change, so check before you set them.

### Short trip: up to 7 days, £79
A trip page for a short trip: up to 3 stops, every day hour by hour, every cost for your group in £, hotels at three prices, a booking list and 1 round of changes. Ready within 5 working days of your brief.

### Standard: 8 to 16 days, £149 (lead with this one)
The full trip page for a two-week trip: up to 7 stops in one or two countries. The route and flights are worked out, every day is hour by hour, every journey is timed, and every cost is added up for your group, with hotels at three prices. Includes entry notes for UK passports, a list of what to book now and what to check later, and 2 rounds of changes. Ready within 5 working days.

### Big trip: 17+ days or 3+ countries, £229
For the long one: as many stops as the trip needs, overland and open-jaw routes, and another way to do the route with the cost of each. Everything in Standard, plus 3 rounds of changes.

**Questions to ask the buyer** (paste into Thatch's intake form):
1. Where would you like to go? A country, a region, or "somewhere warm with ruins" is fine.
2. When, and for how many days including travel?
3. Who's going? Adults, and children with their ages.
4. Which airport or city will you fly from?
5. What's your total budget for the trip, not counting this fee?
6. Pace: relaxed, balanced or packed?
7. What do you love? History, food, beaches, hiking, wildlife, nightlife, diving…
8. Hotels: simple, mid-range or a treat?
9. Anything to see, avoid, or that's already booked? Any mobility needs?

## Etsy: ready-made trip pages

On Etsy, sell **ready-made** routes as a digital download (the trip page as one HTML file, plus a PDF of the summary). Custom work is better sold on Thatch or your own site.

Make each one with `/travel-plan` for 2 travellers from London, then turn it into a site page with `to-site.mjs` without `--sample`. The buyer gets a link and the downloaded file.

Start with routes people search for: **2 Weeks in Vietnam**, **10 Days in Japan**, **2 Weeks in Mexico (Yucatán)**, **2 Weeks Central America: Mexico, Belize, Guatemala**, **1 Week in Portugal**.
